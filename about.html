Our story

A marketplace built around one idea: know her name.

Nileva was started to close the distance between Sudan's women producers and the kitchens of Western Europe — so that behind every jar and packet there is a name, a place, and a fair price.

Why we started Nileva

Sudan produces some of the world's finest gum arabic, hibiscus and sesame — most of it grown, tapped or dried by women working in small cooperatives. For decades, that work has passed through many hands before it reaches a shelf in Europe, and very little of its value ever reaches the woman who did it. Nileva exists to shorten that chain.

We work directly with cooperatives across Kordofan, the Blue Nile, the Nile Valley and Darfur, paying producers at the point of harvest and tracking, openly, how much of each sale reaches them.

What makes us different

Direct trade

We buy straight from cooperatives, cutting out the layers of middlemen that usually separate a harvest from its price.

Open pricing

Every product page shows what the woman who made it was paid, and what covers shipping, packing and customs.

A name, not just a product

Scan the code on any pack and meet the woman behind it — her region, her cooperative, her story.

Built in Finland, for Europe

We're based in Helsinki and ship across Western Europe, handling customs and delivery so cooperatives don't have to.
