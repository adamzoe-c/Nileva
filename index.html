How your order reaches you

From her hands in Kordofan to your kitchen in Helsinki.

01

She grows and harvests

Fatima and her cooperative tap and sort gum arabic by hand, the way her mother taught her. She's paid on collection, not on delivery.

02

We verify and pack in Sudan

Our partner cooperative weighs, records and packs each batch, linking it to the woman who produced it and what she was paid.

03

It ships to Helsinki, then to you

Batches arrive at our Finland warehouse, where we fulfil orders across Western Europe with full customs handling.

04

You scan, and you meet her

A QR code on the pack opens her page — her name, her region, a photo, and exactly how your purchase reached her.

Fatima · Kordofan, Sudan
Meet the woman behind your order

"This harvest is paying for my daughter's school fees."

Fatima has tapped gum arabic trees near her village in Kordofan for eleven years. She leads a cooperative of nine women who sort and grade every batch by hand before it leaves the region.

This is the story that opens when a customer scans the code on her batch — written with her, in her words, and updated each season.
