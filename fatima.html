Fatima Al-Nour

Fatima has tapped acacia trees for gum arabic since she was a girl, learning the craft from her mother in the sands of Kordofan.

Her story

Fatima grew up in a small village in Kordofan, where tapping acacia trees for gum arabic has passed from mother to daughter for generations. She joined the Kordofan Gum Arabic Cooperative twelve years ago and today helps coordinate the harvest across fifteen households. When a Nileva order includes gum arabic, there's a good chance it passed through her hands before it reached the jar.

How your purchase helps

Since Nileva began buying directly from her cooperative, Fatima has been paid at the point of harvest instead of waiting months for a buyer further down the chain.

  • Bought a water pump that cuts her daily walk to the well in half.
  • Covered her eldest daughter's school fees a full term in advance.
  • Helped the cooperative plant 200 new acacia saplings for future harvests.
