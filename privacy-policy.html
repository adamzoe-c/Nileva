Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Nileva collects, why, and the rights you have over it under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Nileva Oy, registered in Finland, is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@nileva.com.

What we collect

When you browse, order or contact us, we may collect: your name, email, delivery and billing address, phone number, order history, and technical data such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your data to process and deliver orders, handle payments, respond to enquiries, prevent fraud, and — with your consent — send you updates about new harvests and cooperatives. We never sell your data.

Legal basis

We process your data to perform our contract with you (fulfilling orders), to comply with legal obligations (such as tax records), and, where you've agreed, on the basis of your consent (marketing emails).

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the site and cart, and optional analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser at any time.

Sharing your data

We share data only where necessary: with payment providers to process transactions, with delivery partners to ship your order, and with our cooperatives in Sudan solely to link your purchase to the woman who produced it — never your personal contact details.

Data retention

We keep order data for as long as required by Finnish accounting law, and account data for as long as you keep an account with us. You can ask us to delete data we're not legally required to keep.

International transfers

Your order and delivery data is processed within the EU/EEA. Where a service provider is located outside the EU/EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@nileva.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; changes take effect once published on this page.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us at hello@nileva.com or through our contact page.
