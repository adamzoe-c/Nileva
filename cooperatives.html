Our cooperatives

The cooperatives behind every product

Nileva works with four women-led cooperatives across Sudan. Together they represent the people, the regions, and the harvests you're supporting with every order.

Kordofan Gum Arabic Cooperative

Kordofan · Gum arabic · 18 women members

Based around El Obeid, this cooperative taps gum arabic from acacia trees across the surrounding savanna. Members include Fatima Al-Nour and Mariam Adam.

Blue Nile Women's Collective

Blue Nile · Sesame & peanuts · 14 women members

This collective grows, hulls and processes sesame and peanuts near the Blue Nile, sharing equipment and labour across the harvest season. Members include Amira Hassan and Nafisa Bakhit.

Nile Valley Hibiscus Growers

Nile Valley · Hibiscus · 9 women members

Growing hibiscus along the Nile Valley, this group hand-dries every flower to preserve its colour and flavour. Members include Halima Idris.

Darfur Basket Weavers

Darfur · Handwoven baskets · 7 women members

A small weaving group in a Darfur village, working from palm-leaf patterns passed down through generations. Members include Zainab Yousif.
